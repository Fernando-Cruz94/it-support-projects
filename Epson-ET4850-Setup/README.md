## 🪛 Setup Steps

### 1. Power and Ink Installation

- Plug in the printer and turn it on.
- Fill each ink tank (Black, Cyan, Magenta, Yellow).
- Start the automatic ink charging process (10–15 minutes).

### 2. Connect to Wi-Fi and PC

- Use the touchscreen to connect the printer to your Wi-Fi.
- On Windows 11:  
  `Settings > Bluetooth & Devices > Printers & Scanners > Add device`

### 3. Update Firmware

- Go to: `Settings > Maintenance > Firmware Update`
- Confirm and follow on-screen instructions.

---

## 🧪 Troubleshooting

### Printing issues (printer detected but not responding):

- Restart the Print Spooler (`services.msc`) and clear spool files.
- In Acrobat Reader: enable **Print as Image** under Advanced print settings.

---

## 📡 Scanner Setup

- Activate scan from printer panel: `Scan > Computer > Search Again`
- Register the printer via [Epson Connect](https://www.epsonconnect.com/activation)
- Download [Remote Print Driver](http://support.epson.net/rpdriver)
- Use **Windows Scan** or **Epson ScanSmart** to complete the scanning process.

---

## 📱 Mobile Setup
